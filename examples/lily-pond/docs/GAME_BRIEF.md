# LILY POND: Game Brief

**Pitch:** Hold to charge a frog's leap, release to land on the next lily pad. Land dead-centre to chain a combo.

**Hook / difference:** Stick-bridge and stack-timing games ask for *length* by holding; here the charge is a visible *arc preview that wobbles*, the pads drift and shrink, and a centre landing rings a bell that climbs a pentatonic scale. Closest games: Stick Hero, Ballz-style jumpers, Crossy-style hoppers. Ours differs by the ink-wash look, drifting pads and the musical combo.

**Core loop:** hold (charge, arc grows) -> release (leap) -> land (centre = PERFECT ring + chime, edge = scrape, miss = splash) -> pad count rises, pads get smaller and start to drift.
**Fail:** splash in the water (one life). **Score:** pads reached; PERFECT streak adds bonus. Best score saved.

**First 10 seconds:** title shows the frog on a pad, a pulsing red ring on the next pad and the word "HOLD". Bot/attract mode plays behind it. First hold teaches everything; no text walls.

**Progression:** 0-30 s static pads, wide. 30 s-2 min pads shrink and drift sideways. 2 min+ a pad sinks after you leave it; every 10th pad is a tiny red "lucky" pad worth 3.

**Out of scope:** shop/skins, levels, multiplayer, story, ads inside gameplay.

**Assumptions (no questions asked):** targets Poki + CrazyGames + YouTube Playables + itch; portrait 9:16 primary, works landscape; mouse/touch/Space; 30-90 s runs, instant restart.
