# LILY POND: Art Bible

**Direction:** Sumi-e ink wash on cream paper with a single vermilion accent (lineage 10), no gradients except ink-wash blotches.

**Palette (roles):** paper `#efe6d2` background | ink `#1d1b1a` outlines + frog | wash `#8a9a8e` (grey-green) water blotches | pad `#5f7a63` pad fill, `#3f5a45` pad rim | red `#c8321f` ONLY for the frog's mark, the target ring and PERFECT | gold-ink `#b08d3c` combo numerals.
**Type:** Shippori Mincho / a serif brush-like OFL face (bundled woff2, latin subset). Numerals large, light weight.
**Outline rule:** ink outlines are irregular (2-4 px, wobble by seeded noise), never perfectly uniform. Fills are slightly offset from outlines (print/brush misregistration 2 px).
**Texture:** paper grain overlay (static noise canvas, 6% opacity) + soft vignette; water drawn as a few wide translucent wash strokes that drift slowly.
**Easing:** slow ease-in-out for the world; frog squash is the only snappy element. Ripples expand in easeOutCubic.

**Do:** empty space as composition; one red thing on screen at a time; brush-trail on the leap.
**Don't:** neon, glow, purple-blue gradients, emoji, perfectly round UI pills with drop shadows.
