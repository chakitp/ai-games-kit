# Art direction starters

Each is a named lineage with concrete constraints. Offer three that fit the game's mood; adapt, don't copy a specific copyrighted work.

1. **Risograph two-ink print**: 2-3 spot inks (e.g. fluorescent pink + teal on cream paper), halftone dot shading, slight misregistration (1-2 px offset on one layer), paper grain overlay. Chunky condensed display type. Motion: snappy, stamped.
2. **Sunday-comics flat vector**: thick uniform outlines (3-4 px at 720p), 4-color limited fills + one spot accent, off-white background, speech-bubble UI. Motion: bouncy with squash and stretch.
3. **1-bit / 2-bit handheld**: 160x144-ish logical grid, 4 shades of one hue (olive, amber, or icy blue), dithered gradients, bold bitmap font. Motion: stepped, 8-12 fps animation feel with 60 fps input.
4. **Woodblock / ukiyo-e inspired**: flat color areas, bold ink outlines, wave/cloud pattern motifs, muted indigo + vermilion + ochre. Motion: slow, flowing, ease-in-out.
5. **Thrift-store packaging**: cardboard texture, mismatched sticker labels, taped corners, stamped numerals, kraft + red + black. Motion: clunky, sliding, rubber-stamp pops.
6. **Chunky clay / toy diorama** (3D or faux-3D): soft fat shapes, single warm key light, shallow depth of field blur on background, limited saturated palette. Motion: heavy, wobbly settle.
7. **Blueprint / technical drawing**: cyanotype blue paper, white line work, dimension callouts as UI, stamped annotation text. Motion: precise, linear-then-snap.
8. **Retro arcade cabinet airbrush**: chrome gradients limited to one object, starfield, scanlines, sticker-sheet UI. Use sparingly; avoid the neon-gradient default by choosing a warm sunset palette and a visible CRT curve and noise.
9. **Papercut diorama**: layered paper with drop shadows between layers (real depth order), pastel palette, torn edges. Motion: parallax, slight sway.
10. **Sumi-e ink + single red**: black ink wash on cream, one red accent for the player or goal, empty space as composition. Motion: slow brush trails.

For each pick, write into the art bible: palette hexes with roles, font names (OFL), outline/shadow rule, texture overlay, easing family, 3 do and 3 don't.
